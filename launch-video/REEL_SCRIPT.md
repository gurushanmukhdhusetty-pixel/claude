# Cinematy — Launch Film Script (v3)

**Format:** 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · **2:00**
**Track:** Stromae – *Alors On Danse* (Radio Edit), cut **0:32.33 → 2:32.33** of the song (continuous, no music edits)
**Tempo:** ≈ 119.8 BPM → 1 beat ≈ 0.50 s · 1 bar ≈ 2.0 s · every cut on the grid
**Style:** pure motion graphics — ink #000 on paper #FFF, Signal Red #D92D20 only for LIVE / unread / deadlines. Instrument Serif (display, never bold, one italic word), Geist (UI), Geist Mono (labels). Logo used exactly as supplied. No AI imagery.
**UI:** every screen is rebuilt as clean vector UI from the real app (logged-in demo), driven by an animated cursor that clicks on beats.
