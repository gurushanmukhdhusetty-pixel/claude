01

What Cinematy is

Cinematy is a home for Hyderabad’s filmmakers: one place where writers, directors, actors, crew and film lovers find each other and get work made. Our line is “Where Hyderabad’s films begin.”

Today most of the city’s indie film work runs on forwarded WhatsApp messages: “anyone know a DOP free this weekend?”, “need a sound recordist urgent”. Cinematy replaces that with a proper platform.

02

What the unit is

The Cinematy Content Unit at MU is a small student crew that makes the content Cinematy posts on YouTube, Instagram and our other channels. You are the people behind the camera, in front of it, and running the shoot.

It’s a crew, not a job.

The deal

Pro bono, not a paid gig

This isn’t a job or an internship. Nobody is paid, including the people who run it.

In return

Credit, portfolio, crew

Your name on everything you make, real work you can show, and people to make it with.

Why it exists

  • Cinematy needs content that sounds like the people it’s for, and filmmakers telling filmmakers’ stories does that better than an agency could.
  • Students who want to make films need a reason, a team and an audience. The unit gives you all three.
  • Every piece you make doubles as proof of what Cinematy stands for: finding your people and getting work made.

04

What you do

Most pieces need several of these roles. You can hold more than one, and we encourage trying a new one once you’re settled.

Writing

Words first
  • Pitch ideas and turn approved ones into scripts, hooks and on-screen text.
  • Write reel scripts that land in the first two seconds.
  • Draft captions in the Cinematy voice.

You hand over: a locked script and caption before the shoot.

Directing

Owns the vision
  • Shape the script into shots: plan the shot list and the look.
  • Run the set creatively and direct the actors.
  • Give notes in the edit until the piece is right.

You hand over: a shot list before the shoot, and sign-off on the final cut.

Acting

On camera
  • Prepare lines and character before the shoot.
  • Show up on time, camera-ready, and take direction.
  • Host explainers and appear in skits, shorts and reels.

You hand over: your performance, and a signed OK for us to post it.

Shooting

Camera & light
  • Plan the camera, lenses and lighting with the director.
  • Shoot clean, well-exposed footage and good audio.
  • Grab extra B-roll and behind-the-scenes clips on every shoot.

You hand over: organised footage in the shared drive the same day.

Editing

Makes it move
  • Cut to the beat of licensed music, with captions on screen.
  • Deliver vertical (9:16) and horizontal (16:9) versions where needed.
  • Apply the Cinematy look: type, colour and the end card.

You hand over: a review cut, then the final export.

Shoot management

Runs the day
  • Build the call sheet: who, where, when and what to bring.
  • Keep the shoot on schedule and everyone fed and safe.
  • Handle the location, the kit checklist and getting permission to film.

You hand over: a call sheet the day before, and a wrap note after.

Line production

Makes it possible
  • Work out what each piece needs: people, kit, props, locations.
  • Arrange borrowed or rented gear and keep any small costs tracked.
  • Spot problems before they reach the set.

You hand over: a needs list and a go or no-go before each shoot.

Planning

Keeps the calendar
  • Maintain the content calendar: what posts when, on which channel.
  • Schedule shoots around classes, exams and weekends.
  • Track every piece from pitch to published.

You hand over: an up-to-date calendar and tracker every week.

Every piece, from a 20-second reel to a short film, moves through the same six steps.

  1. PitchAnyone can pitch. One line on the idea, the format and why our audience would care.Everyone
  2. Script & planThe script is written and locked, then turned into a shot list. The calendar gets a slot.Writing · Directing · Planning
  3. PrepCast, crew, kit, location and permission are confirmed, and the call sheet goes out the day before.Line production · Shoot management
  4. ShootWe start on time, stay on schedule, capture BTS, and back up footage the same day.Directing · Shooting · Acting · Shoot management
  5. Edit & reviewA review cut goes to the director and unit lead, notes come back, and the cut is finished with captions and the end card.Editing · Directing
  6. PublishCinematy posts from its official accounts, with credits for everyone who worked on it.Planning · Cinematy team

Your unit lead shares the meeting rhythm, the shared drive and the content calendar when you join.

06

The Cinematy look

Cinematy is ink on paper. The interface stays black and white so the films themselves can be the colourful thing on screen.

Ink#000000 · type, logo
Paper#FFFFFF · the page
Graphite#6B6B6B · secondary
Signal Red#D92D20 · LIVE, deadlines only
Display · Instrument Serif
Last take tonight.

Headlines. Never bold, often one word in italic.

Body · Geist
Find your crew.

Captions, body text and buttons.

Labels · Geist Mono
Scene 01 · Take 03

Small uppercase labels, counts and timecodes.

The logo

  • CINEMATY over HYDERABAD between two laurel branches, used exactly as supplied, in ink on light and paper on dark.
  • Never recolour, stretch, crop, redraw or add glows and shadows.
  • Give it clear space equal to the height of the CINEMATY letters on every side.

The voice

Talk like the best person on a shoot: specific, warm, fair, and a little funny at 4 a.m.

Like this

“Night shoot at Tank Bund. We’ll feed you at midnight and drop you home after.”

Not this

“Exciting opportunity for passionate individuals to gain exposure!”

Every piece ends on the Cinematy end card: logo, “Coming soon”, “Join the waitlist” and cinematy.in.

07

Ground rules

Always
  • Credit everyone who worked on a piece, by name and role.
  • Get a clear OK from anyone who appears on camera before we post.
  • Ask permission before filming on campus or at any location.
  • Use music and footage we have the rights to.
  • Keep sets safe: no risky stunts, nobody left stranded after a late shoot.
  • Tell your lead early if you can’t make it. It happens, and early notice saves the shoot.
Never
  • Post unit work on personal accounts before Cinematy publishes it. Share it once it’s live.
  • Speak for Cinematy publicly or make promises on its behalf.
  • Share applicants’ or members’ phone numbers or details outside the team.
  • Use AI-generated people or footage in Cinematy content.
  • Film anyone who has asked not to be filmed.
08

What you get

  • Credit on everything you make, on the post and in the description.
  • Portfolio work made to a real brief, for a real audience, that you can show anywhere.
  • A crew: writers, directors, actors and producers to make your own ideas with.
  • Early access to Cinematy and a front-row seat as it launches in Hyderabad.
  • Room to try new roles. Writers can direct, editors can shoot, anyone can pitch.

And because it’s pro bono, you can step back any time. Just give your lead a heads-up so no shoot is left short.
