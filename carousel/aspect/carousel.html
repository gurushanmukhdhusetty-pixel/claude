1890s – 1940s
Movies started boxy.
1.33 : 1 · Silent era
1.37 : 1 · “Academy”, 1932
Early film frames were nearly square. When sound arrived, the soundtrack took a strip of the film, and Hollywood standardised the slightly-wider Academy ratio. For 20 years, every movie was a box.

Cinematy · Frame by Frame03 / 05
1950s · The TV panic
Then TV stole the box.
TV at home · 4 : 3
CinemaScope · ~2.35 : 1
Audiences stayed home with their square TVs. So studios went wide, giving you something a living room couldn’t. The Robe launched CinemaScope in 1953.
India went wide in 1959 with Guru Dutt’s Kaagaz Ke Phool, our first CinemaScope film.
