01

Who we are

Cinematy is Hyderabad’s community for student filmmakers and film lovers, backed by a web app. Writers, directors, actors, DOPs, editors and crews find each other, make films together and get them seen.

We’re building campus teams across the city and launching the app in February. Mastershot and 7Screens are already doing what we care about most: getting students to make and watch films. We’d like to back that, at scale.

02

What Cinematy brings

01Round up clubs across Hyderabad

We reach out to film and media clubs at colleges across the city through our campus leads and network, and bring their teams to the festival as participants and audience.

02Promote the fest and drive registrations

A full campaign across Cinematy’s Instagram, waitlist community, WhatsApp groups and campus teams, with a dedicated registration link so we can track every sign-up we bring.

03Coordinate celebrity outreach

We work with both clubs on shortlisting, approaching and coordinating a film-industry guest: one shared plan, one point of contact, no duplicated calls.

04Content and coverage

Countdown reels, club spotlights and behind-the-scenes before the fest, plus a recap film after, so the segment lives on beyond the day.

Community partners

Mastershot and 7Screens become Cinematy’s first Community Partners. Club members get early access to the Cinematy app from the third week of January, alongside our waitlist, before the public launch in February.

Early access

Members are among the first on the app: build profiles, find crew, post work.

Featured clubs

Both clubs featured on the app and across our launch content as founding partners.

A city-wide stage

Club projects and members reach students across Hyderabad, not just one campus.

04

Who does what

Mastershot & 7Screens
Run the festival segment: programming, venue, judging and on-ground execution.
Cinematy
Club outreach across Hyderabad, promotion and registrations, celebrity coordination, and content coverage.
Together
One shared plan, a weekly check-in, and joint sign-off on the guest and all public announcements.
Credit
Cinematy credited as Community Partner on festival creatives, posts and on stage, with a short Cinematy moment at the segment.
