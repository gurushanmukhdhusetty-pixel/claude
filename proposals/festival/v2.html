Who we are

Cinematy is Hyderabad’s community for student filmmakers and film lovers, backed by a web app. Writers, directors, actors, DOPs, editors and crews find each other, make films together, and get them seen.

We’re building campus teams across the city and launching in February. Mastershot and 7Screens are bringing colleges from across Hyderabad to the Mahindra University Film Festival. We’d like to help make it the biggest student film festival in the city.

Two clubs, one city, every campus. Let’s make the Mahindra University Film Festival the one Hyderabad’s student filmmakers talk about.

What Cinematy brings

Four reels
01Reel 01 · Reach

Bring clubs from across Hyderabad

We reach out to film and media clubs at colleges across the city through our campus leads and network, and bring their teams in as participants and audience.

02Reel 02 · Buzz

Promote the festival and drive registrations

A full campaign across Cinematy’s Instagram, waitlist community, WhatsApp groups and campus teams.

03Reel 03 · Star power

Coordinate celebrity outreach

One shared plan with both clubs to shortlist, approach and coordinate a film-industry guest, with one point of contact and no duplicated calls.

And the big one

We run the festival’s tech, so the clubs can focus on the films.

04Reel 04 · Technology & digital support

A co-branded festival website, online submissions, and an organiser dashboard, built, hosted and managed by Cinematy.

Festival websiteBuilt, hosted and managed by us: schedule, categories, rules, guests and updates.
Online submissionsColleges and teams register and drop the link to their film, then get an instant confirmation.
Submissions managementAn organiser dashboard to track every entry, check eligibility, manage screening lists and share films with judges.
Tech on the dayHelp with registrations and check-in, screening playlists and digital logistics.
