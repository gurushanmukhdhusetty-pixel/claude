Partnership Proposal

Cinematy × Mastershot × 7Screens

1.About Cinematy

Cinematy is a Hyderabad-based community for student filmmakers and film enthusiasts, supported by a web application. It connects writers, directors, actors, cinematographers, editors and crew members so they can collaborate on projects and showcase their work.

Cinematy is building campus teams across Hyderabad and will launch publicly in February. Mastershot and 7Screens bring colleges from across the city together through their annual film festival. Cinematy proposes to support the festival’s reach, participation and operations.

2.Proposed support

2.1Club outreach across Hyderabad

Cinematy will reach out to film and media clubs at colleges across Hyderabad through its campus leads and network, and invite their members to participate in and attend the festival.

2.2Promotion and registrations

Cinematy will promote the festival and drive registrations through its Instagram, waitlist community, WhatsApp groups and campus teams.

2.3Celebrity outreach coordination

Cinematy will work with both clubs on a single, shared plan to shortlist, approach and coordinate a film-industry guest, acting as one point of contact to avoid duplicated outreach.

2.4Technology and digital support

  • Festival website: Cinematy will build, host and manage a co-branded festival website covering the schedule, categories, rules, guests and updates.
  • Online submissions: a submissions page on the festival website where colleges and teams register and submit the link to their film, and receive an instant confirmation.
  • Submissions management: an organiser dashboard to track entries, check eligibility, manage screening lists and share films with judges.
  • Event-day support: assistance with registrations and check-in, screening playlists and digital logistics.

3.Community partnership

Mastershot and 7Screens will be Cinematy’s first Community Partners. Members of both clubs will receive early access to the Cinematy app from the third week of January, alongside Cinematy’s waitlist members and ahead of the public launch in February.

  • Early access: club members will be among the first users of the app.
  • Featured clubs: both clubs will be featured on the app and in Cinematy’s launch communications.
  • City-wide reach: club projects and members will be visible to students across Hyderabad.
