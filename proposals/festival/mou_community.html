Memorandum of Understanding

Community Partnership · Mastershot × Cinematy × 7Screens

This memorandum records the shared intent of the parties below to work together as community partners. It is written in the spirit of partnership and is not a legal contract.

MastershotFilm club
CinematyHyderabad community for student filmmakers
7ScreensFilm club

1.Purpose

To build a friendly, ongoing relationship between the three parties and to support student filmmaking across Hyderabad.

2.What Cinematy intends to offer

  • Community Partner recognition: Cinematy intends to recognise Mastershot and 7Screens as Community Partners.
  • Early access: members of both clubs may be offered early access to the Cinematy app ahead of its public launch, which is currently planned for early next year.
  • Visibility: Cinematy may feature the clubs and their work on the app and in its communications, where appropriate.
  • Campus collaboration: Cinematy’s campus unit hopes to work alongside both clubs on activities of mutual interest.

3.What Mastershot and 7Screens may consider

  • Sharing with members: letting members know about Cinematy and the early access opportunity, if the clubs feel it is useful to them.
  • Collaboration: exploring joint activities such as screenings, meetups or shoots, where it suits the club.
  • Feedback: passing on any feedback members choose to share about the app.

4.Independence

Mastershot and 7Screens remain fully independent. Nothing in this memorandum affects how either club is run, its members, its events or its decisions.

5.Members’ information

Members may join the Cinematy app individually and by choice. No member information is expected to be shared between the parties without the member’s consent.

6.Working together

The parties intend to stay in touch, keep each other informed, and agree on any joint public announcements before they are made.

7.Duration

This understanding begins on signing and continues for as long as the parties find it useful. It may be revisited, changed or ended at any time by mutual conversation.

This memorandum is an expression of shared intent and goodwill. It is not legally binding and does not create obligations for any party. Each party takes part voluntarily, may adjust its involvement as circumstances change, and may step back at any time by letting the others know.
