Memorandum of Understanding

Community Partnership · Mastershot × Cinematy × 7Screens

This memorandum records the shared understanding between the parties below on working together as community partners. It is written in the spirit of partnership and is not a legal contract.

Mastershot
Cinematy
7Screens

1.Purpose

To bring the members of Mastershot and 7Screens into the Cinematy community, give both clubs a city-wide platform, and work together to grow student filmmaking across Hyderabad.

2.Cinematy will

  • Recognise both clubs as Community Partners: Mastershot and 7Screens will be Cinematy’s first Community Partners.
  • Offer early access: members of both clubs will be offered early access to the Cinematy app ahead of its public launch.
  • Feature both clubs: in the partners section of the Cinematy website, and wherever else appropriate.
  • Work alongside the clubs on campus: Cinematy’s MU campus unit will work in collaboration with both clubs on activities of mutual interest, supporting what they already do.
  • Add value for members: wherever possible and within reason, ensure Cinematy and its activities benefit the clubs’ members.

3.Mastershot and 7Screens will aim to

  • Introduce Cinematy to their members: share early access invitations and updates with club members.
  • Collaborate with Cinematy: work with Cinematy across their events and activities, wherever possible and within reason.
  • Support the MU campus unit: support the content and activities of Cinematy’s MU campus unit, wherever possible and within reason.
  • Promote Cinematy: promote Cinematy across their events and channels, wherever possible and within reason.

4.Working together

  • Each party will name a point of contact.
  • The parties will stay in regular touch.

5.Duration

This understanding begins on signing and will continue for the foreseeable future. It is intended to remain in place through any changes in the leadership of any party, so that the partnership carries on beyond the individuals who sign it. The parties may revisit it together whenever needed.

This memorandum is not legally binding. It is made in good faith and in the spirit of partnership, and each party will make reasonable efforts to follow through on it.
