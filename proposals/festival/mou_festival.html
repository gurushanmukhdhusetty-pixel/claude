Memorandum of Understanding

Film Festival Collaboration · Mastershot × Cinematy × 7Screens

This memorandum records the shared intent of the parties below to collaborate on the annual film festival hosted by Mastershot and 7Screens. It is written in the spirit of partnership and is not a legal contract.

MastershotFilm club, co-host of the festival
CinematyHyderabad community for student filmmakers
7ScreensFilm club, co-host of the festival

1.Purpose

To explore ways of growing the festival’s reach and participation across Hyderabad and making it simpler to run.

2.How Cinematy intends to support

  • Club outreach: Cinematy intends to help reach film and media clubs at other colleges and encourage them to take part in and attend the festival.
  • Promotion: Cinematy may help promote the festival through its channels and community.
  • Guest outreach: Cinematy is open to supporting the clubs’ efforts to approach a film-industry guest, in coordination with them.
  • Technology and digital support: Cinematy intends to help with the festival’s digital needs, which may include a festival website, online submissions, tools for managing entries and support on the day, as agreed between the parties.

3.The role of Mastershot and 7Screens

  • The festival: Mastershot and 7Screens own and run the festival and make all decisions about it.
  • Coordination: the clubs may share festival details and plans with Cinematy as needed, so that support stays aligned.
  • Acknowledgement: the clubs may acknowledge Cinematy’s support in festival communications, as they see fit.

4.Costs

Cinematy’s time and technical work are offered free of charge. Any direct hosting or storage costs would be discussed and agreed with the clubs beforehand.

5.Festival data

The festival and its submissions data belong to Mastershot and 7Screens. Any participant information handled by Cinematy is intended to be used only to support the festival.

6.Working together

The parties intend to stay in regular touch while preparing for the festival and to agree on any joint public announcements before they are made.

7.Duration

This understanding applies to the current edition of the festival. The parties may choose to continue the collaboration for future editions.

This memorandum is an expression of shared intent and goodwill. It is not legally binding and does not create obligations for any party. Each party takes part voluntarily, may adjust its involvement as circumstances change, and may step back at any time by letting the others know.
