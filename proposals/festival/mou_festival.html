Memorandum of Understanding

Film Festival Collaboration · Mastershot × Cinematy × 7Screens

This memorandum records the shared understanding between the parties below on collaborating for the annual film festival hosted by Mastershot and 7Screens. It is written in the spirit of partnership and is not a legal contract.

MastershotFilm club, co-host of the festival
CinematyHyderabad community for student filmmakers
7ScreensFilm club, co-host of the festival

1.Purpose

To grow the festival’s reach and participation across Hyderabad and make it simpler to run.

2.Cinematy will aim to

  • Reach out to clubs across Hyderabad: invite film and media clubs at other colleges to take part in and attend the festival, through its campus units and network.
  • Promote the festival: drive registrations through its Instagram, waitlist community, WhatsApp groups and campus teams.
  • Coordinate guest outreach: work with both clubs on a shared plan to approach a film-industry guest.
  • Provide technology and digital support: a co-branded festival website, online submissions, an organiser dashboard for managing entries, and digital support on the day. The exact scope will be confirmed together.

3.Mastershot and 7Screens will

  • Own and run the festival: programming, categories, rules, venue, judging and on-ground execution, and all decisions about it.
  • Share festival details in good time: schedule, categories, rules and guest details, so the website and promotion stay accurate.
  • Coordinate on outreach: keep Cinematy informed of guest and partner conversations, so efforts are not duplicated.
  • Acknowledge the collaboration: recognise Cinematy as a partner in festival communications.

4.Costs

Cinematy’s time and technical work are offered free of charge. The festival will support the direct hosting and storage costs, which Cinematy will estimate and agree with the clubs beforehand.

5.Festival data

The festival and its submissions data belong to Mastershot and 7Screens. Cinematy will use participant information only to support the festival.

6.Working together

  • Each party will name a point of contact.
  • The parties will stay in regular touch, especially while preparing for each edition of the festival.
  • Public announcements, including guest announcements, will be agreed by all three parties before they go out.

7.Duration

This understanding begins on signing and continues on an ongoing basis, for this and future editions of the festival. The parties may revisit it together whenever needed.

This memorandum is not legally binding. It is made in good faith and in the spirit of partnership, and each party will make reasonable efforts to follow through on it. Any changes will be agreed through open conversation, and any party may step back by informing the others.
