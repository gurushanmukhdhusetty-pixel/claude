Memorandum of Understanding

Film Festival Collaboration · Mastershot × Cinematy × 7Screens

This memorandum records the shared understanding between the parties below on how they will work together on the annual film festival hosted by Mastershot and 7Screens. It is written in the spirit of partnership and is not a legal contract.

MastershotFilm club, co-host of the festival
CinematyHyderabad community for student filmmakers
7ScreensFilm club, co-host of the festival

1.Purpose

To grow the festival’s reach and participation across Hyderabad and make it simpler to run.

2.Cinematy will

  • Reach out to clubs across Hyderabad: invite film and media clubs at other colleges to take part in and attend the festival, through its campus units and network.
  • Promote the festival: drive registrations through its Instagram, waitlist community, WhatsApp groups and campus teams.
  • Coordinate celebrity outreach: work with both clubs on one shared plan to shortlist, approach and coordinate a film-industry guest.
  • Provide technology and digital support: build, host and manage a co-branded festival website with online submissions, an organiser dashboard for managing entries, and digital support on the day.

3.Mastershot and 7Screens will

  • Own and run the festival: programming, categories, rules, venue, judging and on-ground execution.
  • Share festival details in good time: schedule, categories, rules and guest details, so the website and promotion stay accurate.
  • Coordinate on outreach: keep Cinematy informed of guest and partner conversations, so efforts are not duplicated.
  • Acknowledge the partnership: recognise Cinematy as a partner in festival communications.

4.Costs

Cinematy’s time and technical work are offered free of charge. The festival will support the direct hosting and storage costs of the website and submissions, which Cinematy will estimate and share beforehand.

5.Festival data and ownership

The festival belongs to Mastershot and 7Screens, and so does its submissions data. Cinematy will use participant information only to run the festival, and will not use it for any other purpose without the participant’s consent.

6.Working together

  • Each party will name one point of contact.
  • The parties will check in regularly while preparing for the festival.
  • Public announcements, including guest announcements, will be agreed by all three parties before they go out.

7.Duration

This understanding runs from signing until the festival is completed and wrapped up. The parties may continue the partnership for future editions by mutual agreement.

This memorandum is not legally binding. It reflects the good faith and shared intent of all three parties. Any party may step back by informing the others, and the parties will settle any changes through open conversation.
