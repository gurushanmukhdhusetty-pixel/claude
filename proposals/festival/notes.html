Meeting Notes

Mastershot × Cinematy × 7Screens · Partnership proposal

1.About Cinematy

Quick intro if you haven’t heard of us. Cinematy is a Hyderabad community for student filmmakers and film lovers, with a web app behind it.

The idea is simple. Writers, directors, actors, DOPs, editors and crew find each other on Cinematy, make films together, and get their work seen.

Where we are right now

We’ve reached a version of the app we’re happy with. The core platform is built and working.

We’re now raising funds, so we have the capital to run the app properly once it’s live.

Alongside that, we’re setting up Cinematy campus units at the major universities, colleges and campuses across Hyderabad. These units won’t compete with existing clubs; they’ll work in collaboration with each campus’s own film club. We support what the club is already doing by bringing in more people, more projects and a connection to filmmakers across the city.

We hope to have the Cinematy campus network ready for engagements by mid-January, ahead of the early access rollout.

2.Proposed support

There are four things we’d like to handle, so you can focus on the films and the festival itself.

2.1Club outreach across Hyderabad

We’ll reach out to film and media clubs at other colleges through our campus units and network, and invite their members to take part and attend.

2.2Promotion and registrations

We’ll promote the festival across our Instagram, our waitlist community, WhatsApp groups and our campus teams, to drive registrations.

2.3Celebrity outreach coordination

Instead of both clubs reaching out separately, we’d work with you on one shared plan to shortlist, approach and coordinate an industry guest. One point of contact, so the same people don’t get contacted twice.

2.4Technology and digital support

This is the biggest one.

  • Festival website: we’ll build, host and manage a co-branded festival website with the schedule, categories, rules, guests and updates.
  • Online submissions: a submissions page where teams register and drop the link to their film, and they get an instant confirmation.
  • Submissions management: an organiser dashboard for you to track every entry, check eligibility, manage screening lists and share films with the judges.
  • Event-day support: help with registrations, check-in, screening playlists and digital logistics.

On cost: our time and technical work are offered free of charge. The only thing we’d request support for is the direct hosting and storage costs, which we’ll estimate and share beforehand.

3.Community partnership

Mastershot and 7Screens would be Cinematy’s first Community Partners.

Members of both clubs get early access to the Cinematy app from the third week of January, alongside our waitlist members and ahead of the public launch in February.

  • Early access: your members are among the first users of the app.
  • Featured clubs: both clubs are featured on the app and in our launch communications.
  • City-wide reach: your projects and members become visible to students across Hyderabad.

4.Close

That’s the proposal. In short, you run the festival and we help with the reach, the outreach and the tech.

Happy to take questions, or we can go through anything in more detail.

If they ask

  • “What do you get out of this?” Visibility with student filmmakers across Hyderabad right before our launch, and two strong clubs as our first partners.
  • “How much will hosting cost?” Usually very little, since teams submit links rather than uploading files. We’ll share an exact estimate before anything is set up.
  • “Who owns the website and the submission data?” It’s your festival. The website is co-branded, and the submissions data is yours. We just build and run it.
  • “Can you guarantee a celebrity?” We can’t promise a name, but we’ll make the outreach organised, shared and much more likely to land.
  • “When would the website be ready?” Once we agree on categories and rules, we can have it live within a couple of weeks.
